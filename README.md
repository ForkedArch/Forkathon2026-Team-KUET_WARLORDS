## ❔ 𝔜𝔯𝔬𝔟𝔩𝔢𝔪 & 𝔘𝔫𝔡𝔢𝔯𝔰𝔱𝔞𝔫𝔡𝔦𝔫𝔤

### 🚨 Problem Statement

A student moves through university carrying an invisible backpack. Inside it are assignments, deadlines, notes, links, passwords to remember, project ideas, event registrations, documents, screenshots, reminders, unfinished tasks, and dozens of browser tabs.

What's inside your digital backpack?  
Probably a folder called *"Important Stuff"* full of files like:

* `assignment_final.pdf`
* `assignment_final2.pdf`
* `mawa_discrete_CT.pdf`
* `e_kemon_ajob_porashona.pdf`
* `siam_ghumacche.png`
* `turjo_bhaiya_handsome.pptx`
* `hridita_oop_notes.pdf`

And somewhere in your browser history is the link you desperately need. Everything is there, but you can't find anything. There is simply too much scattered information—a complete mess.

### 🎯 KUET_WARLORDS's Understanding

The core issue isn't about finding more ways to store files or bookmark links. The real challenge is answering one simple question: **"What do I actually need to do with all of this right now?"**

When information is scattered across different apps, chats, and folders, students spend more time trying to manage their clutter than actually doing their work. We need a system that cleans up this noise, organizes scattered inputs, and turns them into clear, actionable steps.

## 💡 𝕊𝕠𝕝𝕦𝕥𝕚𝕠𝕟: 𝕋𝕙𝕖 𝔻𝕚𝕘𝕚𝕥𝕒𝕝 𝔹𝕒𝕔𝕜𝕡𝕒𝕔𝕜 𝔼𝕟𝕘𝕚𝕟𝕖

An offline-first, browser-based dashboard designed to collect messy campus inputs, sort priorities automatically, and give students a clear framework to execute their work without cognitive overload.

### 🔑 Key Features

* **⚡ Clutter Status Gauge:** A visual indicator that tracks unresolved tasks and reflects current workload pressure.
* **🎯 Eisenhower Prioritization Matrix:** Automatically places tasks and notes into four actionable quadrants based on urgency and importance.
* **⏱️ Focus Sprint Engine:** A built-in Pomodoro timer linked to active task cards to keep study sessions structured.
* **🚨 Panic Isolation Mode:** A quick filter to isolate high-priority deadlines due within the next 12 hours.
* **🎨 Adaptive Workspace Controls:** Custom interface themes (Dark, Light, Hacker, KUET Red/Blue), font sizing, and a minimal Zen Mode.
* **💾 Data Portability:** Zero-latency performance running entirely in the browser, with full JSON backup import/export and Markdown checklist generation.
